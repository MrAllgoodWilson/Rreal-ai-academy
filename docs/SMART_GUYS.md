# The Smart Guys — Advisory Panel Charter

**What it is.** An internal review method the owner asked for: every significant decision on Rreal AI Academy is examined
through eight "lenses", each modeled on the well-known public philosophy of a top technology leader.

**What it is not.** These are not the real people and nothing here is their actual opinion, endorsement, or words.
Claude writes each lens as its own analysis of how that leader's publicly documented principles would apply.
**No invented quotes.** Never present a lens as something a real person said, and never use their names in marketing.

| Lens | Public philosophy it applies | Asks about every decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Jobs** | Radical focus, simplicity, end-to-end craft, "it just works" | What can we remove? Is the first minute magical? |
| **Zuckerberg** | Social graph, growth loops, ship fast, measure everything | How does this spread from one learner to the next? |
| **Cuban** | Unit economics, sell outcomes, customers over investors | Does each member make money, and do they get a result they'd pay more for? |
| **Musk** | First principles, delete steps, extreme speed, ambition | What's the physics-limited version? Which step can we eliminate? |
| **Huang** | Accelerated computing, platforms and ecosystems, latency | Is it fast at the edge, and does it become a platform others build on? |
| **Krishna** | Enterprise trust, hybrid cloud, governance, responsible AI | Would a Fortune 500 or a government workforce board buy this? |
| **Su** | Execution discipline, performance engineering, roadmap you hit | Is it measured, tested, reliable — and will we ship it on schedule? |
| **Altman** | AI-native products, rapid model progress, safety | Does it get better automatically as models improve — and is it safe? |

## How decisions run
1. State the decision and options in one paragraph.
2. Each lens gives a one-to-three-sentence position and a vote (Yes / No / Conditional).
3. Record disagreements explicitly — they're the useful part.
4. The owner decides. Log it in `docs/DECISIONS.md` with date, choice and the dissenting lens.

The panel can be extended with more lenses (e.g. security, education science, accessibility advocates) on request.
